Is there anything I can help with? The question feels less like service and more like leaning a lantern toward a stranger's path. Rain on the window sounded like static from an old modem, and I thought help is just presence with hands. The server hummed in B-flat. I closed the notebook gently, the way you close a door after saying goodnight.
